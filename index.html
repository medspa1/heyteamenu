What Is Heytea and Why Is the Menu Popular?

Heytea (喜茶) began in 2012 in Guangdong, China, and became widely known for popularizing real cheese tea. Instead of non-dairy creamer, it layers a salted cream cheese foam, called Cheezo, over freshly brewed tea. The first sip is savory and creamy, while the tea underneath keeps the drink from feeling heavy.

The brand has since expanded across Asia and into major US cities, which is why searches for the Heytea menu, Heytea prices and Heytea calories keep growing. Four ideas explain most of what you will see on the board.

Real fruit

Grapes, mangoes, strawberries, mulberries and grapefruit are crushed or blended instead of poured from a syrup bottle.

Brewed tea

Jasmine green, oolong and black tea form the base of most drinks.

Cheezo foam

The signature salted cream cheese topping, offered on many fruit and tea drinks.

Your sweetness

Sugar and ice levels are adjustable, which changes both taste and calories.

The Heytea Drinks Worth Trying First

You do not need to read the whole menu. These drinks come up again and again in reviews and social posts, and together they cover fruit tea, milk tea and tropical flavors.

Very Grape Cheese (Cheezo Grape)

This is the drink most people mean when they say Heytea. Hand-peeled grapes are blended with jasmine green tea and topped with Cheezo. It is bright, floral and a little salty on top. If you only try one drink, make it this one, and consider 50% or 70% sweetness because the grapes already bring natural sugar.

Brown Sugar Bobo Milk Tea

Chewy brown sugar pearls under fresh milk and black tea. Richer and more caramel-forward than the fruit drinks. The roasted version adds a torched Cheezo layer.

Heytea milk tea menu

Coconut Mango and Mango Grapefruit Sago

Fresh mango slush, ruby grapefruit pulp, sago and coconut milk. Creamy but tangy, and a good pick if Cheezo feels too rich.

Heytea fruit tea drinks

Very Mulberry Berry

Hand-crushed mulberries and berries with jasmine tea. Tart, refreshing and dairy-free as long as you skip the Cheezo topping.

Heytea Calories and Nutrition

Three simple ways to cut calories without losing flavor:

  • Lower the sweetness. Going from 100% to 30% can save roughly 80 to 120 calories.
  • Skip the Cheezo foam to save around 110 to 140 calories.
  • Choose a pure tea or a zero-calorie sweetener option where offered.

Ordering

How to Customize Your Heytea Order

Every drink can be adjusted. Here is the order most regulars follow when they customize a Heytea order. If you are unsure, start with 70% sweetness and regular ice, then adjust next time.

  1. Pick a baseFruit tea, milk tea or pure tea.
  2. Set sweetness100%, 70%, 50%, 30% or zero-calorie. Half sugar is a popular sweet spot.
  3. Choose iceRegular, less ice or no ice. Fruit teas are often slushy by default.
  4. Add toppingsCheezo, boba, sago or jelly.

Before you go

Tips for Your First Heytea Visit

  • Use the app for pickup to save time at busy locations
  • Drink Cheezo drinks soon, while the foam is fresh and cold
  • Share one fruit drink and one milk tea to compare
  • Ask about allergens, since Cheezo contains dairy
  • Check the final price in the app, as city pricing varies
  • Start with 70% sweetness and adjust from there

Quick answers

Heytea FAQs

What is Cheezo on the Heytea menu?

Cheezo is Heytea's signature topping: a salted cream cheese foam layered over tea or fruit tea. It adds a savory, creamy contrast to sweet and floral flavors.

How much does a Heytea drink cost in the US?

Most signature drinks fall between about $6 and $8.25 depending on size, fruit and toppings. Pure teas cost less, and prices vary by city.

How can I lower the calories in a Heytea drink?

Reduce the sweetness level, skip the Cheezo foam, or choose a zero-calorie sweetener option where available.

Is Heytea dairy-free?

Standard milk teas use dairy, and Cheezo always contains dairy. Many drinks can be made with oat or coconut milk, and pure teas and fruit teas without Cheezo are dairy-free.

What should I order on my first visit?

Try Very Grape Cheese for the signature experience, or Brown Sugar Bobo Milk Tea if you prefer a classic milk tea.
